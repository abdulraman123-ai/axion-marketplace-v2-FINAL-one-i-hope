# Axion Store Listing Draft — ClientLaunch

## Product title
ClientLaunch: First-Client Toolkit for Freelancers

## Suggested launch price
US$9 one-time for the initial validation period. This is a starting hypothesis, not evidence of willingness to pay. Test with real buyers and adjust based on feedback.

## Short description
A practical client-acquisition starter system for freelancers who can do the work but need help turning one skill into a clear offer, honest portfolio proof, personalized outreach, consistent follow-ups, and clear proposals—without a monthly lead-tool subscription.

## Long description
Finding your first clients can feel confusing when you have a skill but no repeatable way to turn it into paid work. ClientLaunch gives you a simple process to choose a focused service, demonstrate your abilities honestly, research relevant prospects, write tailored messages, track follow-ups, and define the scope of a project before you start.

Built for new freelancers, solo service providers, independent creators, and side-hustlers offering services. It is designed for people who want a practical system rather than a complicated CRM or another recurring software bill.

## What buyers receive
- A 30-day action plan with practical daily steps.
- Offer clarity worksheet to define one service, buyer, problem, and outcome.
- Portfolio proof builder for people without a client testimonial history.
- Outreach template library for first contact, warm referral, professional social messages, and respectful follow-ups.
- Spreadsheet workbook with prospect pipeline, follow-up dates, dashboard, offer builder, and project-pricing calculator.
- Proposal and scope template covering deliverables, timeline, revisions, price, payment terms, and exclusions.
- AI prompt library that buyers may use in their own ChatGPT or Gemini account if they choose; no AI API is included.
- CSV lead tracker and printable/plain-text worksheets.

## Main outcomes
1. Explain the service clearly.
2. Create honest proof of ability before a paid client project.
3. Contact fewer, better-fit prospects with messages based on real observations.
4. Keep track of replies and follow-ups instead of relying on memory.
5. Set expectations before doing the work.

## Ideal buyer
- Beginner freelancers with a skill but no regular client pipeline.
- Video editors, designers, writers, virtual assistants, and other solo service providers.
- Creators testing a service business on a tight budget.

## Requirements
A spreadsheet app compatible with .xlsx is recommended for the tracker. Markdown and CSV files can also be opened in a text editor. Optional AI prompts are copied into an AI product account chosen by the buyer; they are not connected automatically.

## Honest limitations
ClientLaunch does not provide leads, send emails, guarantee replies, guarantee clients, or promise earnings. Buyers must research each prospect, verify every factual claim, respect opt-outs, and comply with applicable laws. The workbook stores its entries locally in the buyer's copy. It does not automatically connect to the hosted demo website.

## FAQ
**Does this automatically find clients?** No. It helps buyers organize and carry out their own focused prospecting.

**Does it send outreach messages?** No. It prepares drafts and follow-up dates; users send messages manually and must respect opt-outs.

**Do I need to buy an AI API?** No. The core worksheets and workbook work without an API. Optional prompts can be pasted into an AI account the buyer already uses.

**Will it guarantee my first client?** No. Results depend on the service, market, proof, outreach quality, timing, and other factors beyond the toolkit's control.
